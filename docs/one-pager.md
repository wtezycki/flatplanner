# Flatplanner: one-pager

Numbers come from [ingest-report.md](ingest-report.md), generated by the import on 2026-11-05.

## Data

- **Source and size:** flat sales in Gdańsk, Gdynia and Sopot from otodom.pl, fetched once. 198 listings fetched, 197 kept (1 rejected: not a flat sale).
- **Split:** Gdańsk 108, Gdynia 59, Sopot 30; primary market 99, secondary 98.
- **Median price per m²:** Gdańsk 12 844 zł, Gdynia 11 741 zł, Sopot 25 450 zł. Sopot is about twice Gdańsk, so "cheap" must be judged against the listing's own city.

## Key assumption: otodom fields already hold what buyers ask about first

Verified by fill-rate per field (share of listings with a value):

| Field | Gdańsk | Gdynia | Sopot |
|---|---|---|---|
| district | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| floor | 86% | 100% | 100% |
| finish condition | 94% | 97% | 100% |
| ownership | 94% | 97% | 73% |
| maintenance fee (czynsz) | 37% | 42% | 43% |
| lift | 71% | 73% | 30% |

- Core fields hold up in all three cities. Two gaps follow from how otodom publishes data: the maintenance fee is missing in about 60% of listings (`Rent = 0` is treated as "no data", never as zero), and lift in Sopot is marked in 30% of listings. Both are shown as "no data", not as "no".
- `Rent = 0` is 15% of secondary-market listings but 68% of primary-market ones, so the fee is least known where developers sell.

## Failure mode: "nice" and "cheap" can push developers down

- Only 1 of 99 primary-market listings is "ready to use" (93 are "to be completed"), against 75 of 98 on the secondary market. A "nice" preference mapped to finish condition would rank developers' listings last.
- "Cheap" against the city median is milder: the primary market is 50% of all listings and 40% (8 of 20) of the cheapest by price per m² relative to the city median.
- Business consequence: lower visibility for the segment that pays for listings. A real portal would correct it with a median per city × market and a better "nice" signal.

## Data quality notes

- 26% of listings (52 of 197) sit in at least one pair that looks like a duplicate (same district, rooms, floor, area within 0.5 m², price within 3%). The ten pairs printed in the report are all agency listings, consistent with units of one development, so these are candidates, not confirmed duplicates. Grouping them is the first item in "with more time".
- 4 listings are flagged as price outliers (price per m² outside 7 000–40 000 zł); 5 have "floor 10 or higher" stored as 11.
- One listing has an advertiser type outside the expected set (`developer`); it is stored as "no data" and flagged, not mapped.
- District labels come from otodom's reverse geocoding and include composite names such as `zaspa--mlyniec`. 25 districts have a single listing; no reference list of administrative districts was used to check them.
